How the recommendation is calculated

Total cost = quantity × unit price − discount + tax on discounted goods + freight.

Suppliers exceeding the delivery limit or falling below the warranty requirement are excluded from scoring. Among eligible suppliers, each criterion is scaled from 0 to 100: lowest cost, shortest delivery and longest warranty score highest. When all values are equal, every supplier receives 100 for that criterion. The weighted average gives the final score.

Scores are relative to the eligible quotations in this comparison. Equal scores are reported as a tie; display order then uses lower cost, shorter delivery and supplier name. Quality, compliance and supplier verification require a separate review.
