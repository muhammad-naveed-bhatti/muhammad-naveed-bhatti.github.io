What it collects

Product data with a clear source.

Identify the product

Product name, SKU and supplier label. Missing fields remain blank, so you can distinguish published information from gaps.

Review listed prices

Exact listed price, currency and availability where provided. Original currencies are preserved.

Keep the evidence

Product URL, source page and collection timestamp travel with every exported record. Double-click a row to check its source.

Get started

Download. Extract. Collect.

STEP 1

Extract the full ZIP

Open SupplierCatalogueCollector.exe from the extracted folder. Keep the included _internal folder beside it.

STEP 2

Try the demo

Click “Load offline demo” for three fictional sample products, or collect the prefilled Books to Scrape practice catalogue.

STEP 3

Export to your spreadsheet

Review the results, then click “Export CSV”. Open the file in Excel or your preferred spreadsheet application.

Supported in this version

  • Books to Scrape practice catalogue, including next-page collection.
  • Public product pages with standard Product JSON-LD data.
  • Multiple URLs, optional supplier label, stop control and duplicate removal.
  • Source links, collection timestamps and Excel-friendly CSV.

Know what to expect

  • Other sites need individual product URLs; automatic pagination is limited to the practice catalogue.
  • Login-only, CAPTCHA-protected and JavaScript-only product data are not supported.
  • Listed prices may exclude tax and delivery. Verify specifications and terms before comparing supplier quotations.
  • The catalogue CSV is a research sheet, not the Quotation Analyzer import template.
